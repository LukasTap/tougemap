Which end is the bad one
Fog: the high end. Temperature: the low. Wind: the low — still air is what lets fog settle and stay. Dew spread: the low. A range prints both ends and leaves you to remember which one you were looking for, differently for every tile.
Retired
Humidity, Dew Point and Dew Spread are the three ingredients the fog score is cooked from. Printing them beside the score is the recipe next to the dish — and they were showing the current hour, not the night. The score and the curve replace all three.
Recommendation
A. The curves already carry the spread — that is their whole job. What a curve cannot give you is an exact figure and the hour it lands on, which is precisely what A adds. Clean division: curve for shape, tile for number and moment.
