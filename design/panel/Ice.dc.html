Why +3, not 0
Tarmac radiates heat to a clear sky faster than the air above it, so the road sits below the reported air temperature — bridges and shaded valley floors first. +3°C air is a live ice possibility; road-weather services trigger frost warnings around +4°C. The conditions driving that are the same ones driving the fog curve.
